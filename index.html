How it works

Four simple steps when every second matters.

Prepare once, then let SilentHalos help you alert trusted contacts and preserve critical incident information when you need it.

01

Prepare before you need it

Set up your trusted contacts, permissions and safety preferences in advance.

02

Trigger an emergency alert

Use your configured emergency trigger, including Shake Detection when enabled, when you believe help may be needed.

03

Share critical information

SilentHalos can share incident information such as your location and alert details with trusted contacts.

04

Preserve incident evidence

Depending on device permissions and feature availability, incident evidence such as audio and camera data may be captured and stored.

Important safety information

SilentHalos supports personal safety. It does not replace emergency services.

SilentHalos is not a police, ambulance, fire, rescue or emergency-dispatch service. In an emergency, contact the appropriate official emergency service whenever you are able to do so. App functionality may be affected by network availability, device settings, permissions, battery level, GPS, internet connectivity and operating-system restrictions.

FAQ

What you should know before launch.

Is SilentHalos an emergency service?

No. SilentHalos is a personal-safety and emergency-assistance tool and does not replace official emergency services.

Who receives an alert?

Alerts are intended for the trusted contacts you configure in the app, according to the features and permissions you enable.

Does SilentHalos work only for driving incidents?

No. SilentHalos is designed for many personal-safety scenarios and should not assume the user is driving unless the incident data indicates that.
